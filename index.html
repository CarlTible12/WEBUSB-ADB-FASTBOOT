WebUSB ADB / Fastboot demo

Requires Chrome or Edge, served over http://localhost or https:// (WebUSB needs a secure context and won't work from a file:// URL).

On Windows, the stock ADB/Fastboot drivers usually claim the USB interface before WebUSB can — you may need a WinUSB-compatible driver (e.g. via Zadig) for the device's ADB/Fastboot interface. Linux and macOS generally work without extra setup.
